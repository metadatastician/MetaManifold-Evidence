# Maintainers

For whoever keeps the two repositories healthy: reviewing, keeping CI meaningful, moving pins, cutting releases.

This section states only what can be checked: who owns what, what CI runs, which rules are enforced, and where the pins live. It does not invent process. MetaManifold-WebUI's own rules are in its [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](https://github.com/JoshuaJewell/MetaManifold-WebUI/blob/257bcdec2db0d4231ed9d5b160b5caa623485681/CONTRIBUTING.md), and they win over anything here.
