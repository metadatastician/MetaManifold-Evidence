https://github.com/metadatastician/standards/blob/main/3-practice/PROOF-POLICY.adoc[standards

=== Supporting lemmas

The census counts the 22 theorems above. The Agda modules also define
helper lemmas that those proofs use, and they are not graded separately:
`≤-refl`, `≤-trans`, `≤-total`, `≤-+ʳ`, `+-monoʳ`, `+-cancelʳ`, `+-assoc`,
`+-zero`, `+-suc`, `+-comm`, `subst`, `≤-diff`, `<-true`, `<-false` in
`Counts.agda`, and `≤⇒lo` with the private `irrefl`, `zero-not-suc`, `≤0`
and `⊥-elim` in `Bounds.agda`. They are checked wherever the theorems that
use them are checked. Their counterparts in the recheck legs differ:

* Lean re-proves them by hand, with no axioms, mostly under the same
  name. There are three exceptions, all recorded in `lean/CORRESPONDENCE.adoc`:
  `zero-not-suc` is re-proved as `suc≰zero`; `⊥-elim` is Lean core's
  `False.elim`, so nothing is ported; and `subst` takes a `Prop`-valued
  motive where Agda's takes `Set p`, which is weaker but enough for its only
  uses, at `≤` motives.
* Rocq re-proves the order lemmas (`le_refl`, `le_trans`, `le_total`,
  `le_plus_r`, `plus_mono_r`, `plus_cancel_r`, `le_diff`, `ltb_true`,
  `ltb_false`, `irrefl`, `le0`, `le_to_lo`), all closed under the global
  context.
* Rocq takes the four addition identities (`+-assoc`, `+-zero`, `+-suc`,
  `+-comm`) from its standard library (`Nat.add_assoc` and so on). Those
  are counterparts used inside the Rocq proofs, not rechecks of the Agda
  lemmas.

If any helper is promoted to a theorem in its own right, it joins the
census with its own grade.

`julia-agrees` is a certificate about one repository's Julia code, so it
reads as engineering under PROOF-POLICY §1. It is carried on the theory
ladder because the owner asked for every theorem to have both recheck legs.
Outside the 0..12 grid the Julia code is tested, not proved.

== Independent statement review (2026-11-09)

A reviewer who wrote neither leg compared each of the 22 Rocq and Lean
statements with its Agda statement, after unfolding definitions. It found
all 22 matching in both legs, none weaker, stronger or vacuous. The checks
were partly by kernel and partly by reading:

* Kernel: the elaborated statements were printed (`Set Printing All`,
  `#check`/`#print`).
* Kernel: `verdict`, `lo`, `hi`, `min`, `span` and `naturals` take the same
  values in Agda (`refl`), Rocq and Lean over finite domains (y, n up to 25),
  with a negative control.
* Kernel: the ported checker's `report` agrees with Agda's on 120
  configurations outside the 546-row explorer table.
* Byte comparison: all four generated tables are identical to independent
  normalisations of the Agda tables.
* By reading: quantifier structure, premises, the direction of each
  implication, Σ versus `sig` or subtype, and every ported definition
  clause by clause.

Only two stale sentences in the correspondence files were found, and both
are now corrected. This review is an agent's, so it informs the owner's
decision and does not replace it. The legs stay `BRIDGE-PENDING` until
the owner records `RECONCILED`.

== The finite checker port

The bridge theorems relate the counts model to the finite checker of
`hyperpolymath/residual-evidence-types`, whose Agda checker is proved equal
to the explorer's 546-row table by `refl`. Both recheck legs port that
checker, and each proves its port equal to the same 546-row table, generated
from `tests/correspondence/ExplorerTable.agda` at the pinned commit. So the
three checkers agree on every row the explorer emits. That is agreement on
that table, not a proof that the definitions are equal.
