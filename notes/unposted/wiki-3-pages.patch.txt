+### Split deployment
+
+Serving the frontend from another origin than the API needs two settings:
+`apiBase` in `frontend/public/config.json` (the API's address) and
+`JULIA_METAMANIFOLD_ALLOWED_ORIGINS` (the frontend's address) when starting the
+server. Since 2026-11-13 the frontend validates `apiBase`: it must be an
+absolute `http://` or `https://` URL, optionally with a path prefix, and no
+user name, password, query or fragment. A refused value is reported in the
+browser console and the frontend falls back to its own origin. The README's
+"Split deployment" section is the reference.
